Hi, I'm Lorenz Saß. I was born in Hamburg in 1996 and grew up in Schwarzenbek, a small town near Hamburg. In 2010, I moved with my family to Qualitz, a small village in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, where I finished middle school.

In 2013, I moved to Rostock, a coastal city on the Baltic Sea, to complete my A-levels. Three years later in 2016, I moved to Berlin to begin my apprenticeship as a Software Developer at the TU-Berlin. I've been living in Berlin-Spandau ever since.
