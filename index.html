Hi, I'm Lorenz Saß. I was born in Hamburg in 1996 and grew up in Schwarzenbek, a small town near Hamburg. In 2010, I moved with my family to Qualitz, a small village in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, where I finished middle school.

In 2013, I moved to Rostock, a coastal city on the Baltic Sea, to complete my A-levels. Three years later in 2016, I moved to Berlin to begin my apprenticeship as a Software Developer at the TU-Berlin. I've been living in Berlin-Spandau ever since.

Hobbies

Game Jams

I enjoy participating in game jams with a good friend of mine. We regularly take part in Ludum Dare, a game jam that takes place approximately every six months. So far, we have participated five times! The concept is simple but challenging: a theme is announced, and we have just 72 hours to develop a game inspired by it. This requires creativity, teamwork, and the ability to turn an idea into a playable experience within a very limited time. Music Festivals

I enjoy attending all kinds of electronic dance music (EDM) festivals. For me, they are a great way to discover new music, enjoy the atmosphere, and meet people who share the same passion. This year, I visited Defqon.1, Airbeat One, and Nation of Gondwana. Each festival offered a different experience, and I especially love feeling the bass vibrate through my hair and witnessing the incredible light shows that make every festival unforgettable.

Favorite Artist: D-Block & S-Te-Fan

Gardening (Beginner)

Since moving into my current place, I've had a balcony that's big enough to start my own little gardening project. This is my first year growing plants, and I've been experimenting with different types of chili peppers. Some of them are already looking great and are almost ready to be harvested! It's not much yet, but watching something grow from a tiny plant into something I can actually harvest is surprisingly rewarding. You know what they say: it's not much, but it's honest work! :)

Astronomy

I've been fascinated by space and science fiction for as long as I can remember. Eventually, this fascination led me to astronomy as a hobby. Together with my father, I bought a 6-inch Dobsonian telescope as a little project, and we started manually hunting for planets in our solar system. There is something truly incredible about finally finding a planet in the night sky and seeing it with your own eyes. It reminds you that what you're looking at is an actual distant world, not just a photograph in a book or on a screen.

So far, we've managed to observe almost every planet in our solar system, with only Uranus and Mercury still missing from our list. Our next goal is to get into astrophotography and capture some of what we see. That will take a bit more time, practice, and equipment, but we're looking forward to the challenge!

Fun fact: With 200x magnification, you can count the holes in the weather vane on top of a church about 500 metres away.
